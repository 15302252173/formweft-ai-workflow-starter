# 20 business workflow practice cards / 20个业务工作流练习

A free FORMWEFT learning collection: 20 distinct tasks, fictional inputs, bilingual prompts and reviewer answer keys. These are authored exercises, not customer results or live AI evaluations.

20 个不同业务场景，每份附虚构输入、可复制中英请求、参考答案与变式。选择一个场景，只把任务、输入和请求交给 AI，再用答案复核。
