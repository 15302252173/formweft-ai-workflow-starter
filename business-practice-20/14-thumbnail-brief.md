# 视频封面简报 / Draft a thumbnail brief

FORMWEFT practice card · 14/20 · 2026-09-14

**Fictional teaching data / 全部为虚构教学数据。** This is a copyable exercise, not a customer case study or a ready-made automation. 本页不连接业务系统，也不自动发送消息。

## Task / 任务

Create a design brief from supplied facts and constraints. 把素材与目标转成封面简报，不冒充完成设计或测试。

Input fields / 输入字段：`video_topic, audience, approved_title, supplied_assets, constraints, output_size`

## Sample inputs / 样例输入

1. Topic: editing existing SRT captions. Audience: first-time users. Title: 'Edit your first caption'. Assets: one genuine editor screenshot.
2. Constraints: no customer logos, no invented before/after results, title under 6 English words.
3. Requested output: internal thumbnail brief only.

## Expected review / 预期答案

1. Use the supplied screenshot; title has 4 words and fits the constraint.
2. Specify focal area, title placement, contrast and a crop check.
3. Do not state that a thumbnail image or A/B test has already been produced.

**Key check / 关键检查：** Output a composition brief with asset references and a review list. 输出可交给设计者的要求。

## Change one input / 变式练习

Remove the screenshot from inputs: request a permitted source image; do not invent an authentic product screenshot.
